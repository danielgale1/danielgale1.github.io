a bit about me

A website is step one

Since 2023 I've been building websites for clients, front end and back end, and running the marketing around them: social media, ads and SEO. Early on I noticed the site was rarely the hard part. The trouble was what came after: nobody could find it, or the leads came in and nobody followed up.

So now I handle the whole path. I design and build the site, get it found on Google and in AI search, run the social media and ads that bring people in, and connect every form to a CRM that follows up automatically. One person, so nothing gets lost between handoffs, and I explain all of it in plain language.

AI is part of my daily workflow: ChatGPT, Claude and Claude Code for research, content and debugging. They make me faster. I still make the calls.
