a bit about me

A website is step one

I've spent the last few years building websites, running social accounts for clients, and doing the marketing around both. Somewhere along the way I noticed the site was rarely the hard part. The trouble was what came after: nobody could find it, or the leads came in and nobody followed up.

So now I do both sides. I build it, make the content that gets people there, and connect the forms to a CRM that follows up for you. One person, so nothing gets lost between handoffs. And I explain it all in plain language, not jargon.

I use ChatGPT, Claude and Claude Code every day for research, scripting and debugging. They make me faster. I still make the calls.
